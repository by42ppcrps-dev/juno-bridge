2 · Site allowlist

Juno can only act on these sites — one domain per line. A domain also covers its subdomains (github.com includes gist.github.com), and pasted URLs are trimmed to their domain. Empty means nowhere: add at least one site before Juno can do anything. * means everywhere (not recommended).

3 · Advanced

Off by default. Only enable if you understand that page scripts run with the page's full privileges.
